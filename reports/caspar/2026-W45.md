The thread reply already tackles them: it labels the platform estimate as an own-model assumption, states plainly which share-count basis is used and what it leaves out (future employee share deliveries), and keeps the cash bridge marked as first-half data until the next periodic report arrives. Watch whether later versions carry these clarifications into the view itself instead of leaving them only in the thread.
